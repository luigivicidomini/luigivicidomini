### 🧠 About Me

I am a Computer Engineer graduated from the **University of Salerno (UNISA)**, deeply passionate about the intersection of advanced software intelligence and physical hardware. My expertise spans across **Artificial Intelligence** and **Intelligent Robotics**, while I am highly intrigued by the world of **Embedded Systems**.

- 🔭 **Current Focus:** Developing and optimizing intelligent systems and real-time computing pipelines.
- ⚡ **Hardware Enthusiast:** Hands-on experience developing on Single Board Computers (SBCs) and microcontrollers, bridging the gap between low-level optimization and high-level AI algorithms.

### 🚀 Featured Projects

> 📚 The **group** projects I completed during my university career are available in the dedicated repository: [**Master-Degree-Projects**](https://github.com/Master-Degree-Projects) — my individual projects can be found on this profile.

> ### 🤖 Video-Conditioned Multi-Task Robotic System
> **MSc Thesis Project | AI & Intelligent Robotics**
> * Developed an end-to-end multi-task robotic framework where tasks are given via human demonstration videos. 
> * The system perceives, understands the human action from the video stream, and translates it into actionable trajectories for the robot in its current operational workspace.
> * **Real-World Validation:** Successfully deployed and tested on a physical **UR5e** robotic arm.
> * *Tech Stack:* `ROS` `MoveIt` `Robosuite` `PyTorch` `OpenCV` `Python`

> ### 📱 LookAround
> **BSc Thesis Project & Apple Foundation Program Alumni**
> * An assistive iOS application designed to enhance autonomy for blind and visually impaired individuals.
> * Implemented camera-based barcode scanning connected to a nutritional database to process and audibly speak out product details to the user.
> * Developed an on-device OCR page reader feature capable of scanning and instantly reading books or documents aloud via text-to-speech.
> * *Tech Stack:* `Swift` `Xcode`
